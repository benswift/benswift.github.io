**Research background.** Generative AI models are usually met one step at a
time: a prompt in, an image or a caption out. _PANIC!_ wires them end-to-end
into a feedback loop instead. Text becomes an image, the image is captioned
back into text, and on it goes. The work sits where interactive installation
meets machine-learning research. Its question is what the long run of such a
loop reveals: where meaning drifts, and where the models' biases surface as
outputs become inputs.

**Research contribution.** In _PANIC!_, visitors compose a chain of generative
models and watch the semantic drift play out live. Outputs stream to gallery
displays, including a Vestaboard split-flap panel. The work contributes both
an artefact and a method. The artefact is a reconfigurable, public-facing
instrument for probing multi-model feedback. The method is in the companion
paper, which uses persistent homology to measure the shape of the loop's
trajectories. Making the loop playable turns a technical curiosity into
something an audience can interrogate directly.

**Research significance.** The work was peer-selected for the New Interfaces
for Musical Expression conference (NIME 2025) and shown at SXSW Sydney 2024
and Australian Cybernetic 2022, after a first outing at Creative AI
Sydney 2022. It is now a permanent install in the Cybernetic Studio. Its
significance rests on that curated presentation at distinguished venues, on
the peer-reviewed companion analysis, and on its life as a citable,
openly-archived output.
