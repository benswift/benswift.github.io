Hi, I'm Dr. Ben Swift. I build things that make AI legible to the people it
affects: students, teachers, public servants, and anyone else who'd like to know
what's going on under the hood. I lead the Cybernetic Studio at the
[ANU School of Cybernetics](https://cybernetics.anu.edu.au/), where our
Human-Scale AI program slows computation down and blows it up until you can get
your hands on it.

The best-known of those things is
[LLMs Unplugged](https://www.llmsunplugged.org/), where you build a language
model with pen, paper and dice. I also teach
[_Agentic Coding Studio_](https://comp.anu.edu.au/courses/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/),
a course on shipping real software with AI coding agents like Claude Code, and I
make open-data and art projects like the
[APS AI Tracker](https://apsaitracker.app) and
[Slop University](https://slop.university). Before all that I was a
[livecoder](/livecoding/), and I still am when the day job lets me. There's more
on my [research](/research/) page, my [code](https://github.com/benswift) is on
GitHub, and sometimes I write about all of this on my [blog](/blog/).

I care a lot about making computing a welcoming, interesting and creatively
satisfying place, especially for people who don't fit the traditional picture of
a "computer scientist".

If you'd like to get in touch---and please do!---you can email me at
[ben.swift@anu.edu.au](mailto:ben.swift@anu.edu.au).
