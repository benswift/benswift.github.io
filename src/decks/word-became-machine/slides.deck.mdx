I build software now\

![bg](./assets/bg-word-machine.avif)

```notes
- I built this site and the sheet tool mostly by talking to one
- 20 years of code where the machine does exactly what you typed; now I say it in English and it interprets, decides, builds --- and it runs code, changes files
- feels like managing people (the centurion), but they aren't people; not settling what they are
- **land this:** first time in my life I've spoken to a non-human part of creation in plain English and had it understand well enough to act
- (if your dog answered back --- hold that for Balaam)
- scripture's words to non-persons are almost all God's → five verses
```

“And God said, Let there be light:\
**and there was light**”

<span class="verse-ref">Genesis 1:3, BBE</span>

![bg](./assets/bg-witness.avif)

```notes
- baseline: first word in scripture spoken to no one, and it happens (Ps 33:9, 148:8)
- feeling: "let there be a login page" and there is; used to be bricklaying; Adam could only name (Gen 2:19)
- distance: not from nothing, built from others' words, often not what I meant
- deepens wonder, or makes it deceptively familiar?
```

{/* _class: hero */}

“And Balaam said to the ass,\
**You have made me seem foolish**”

<span class="verse-ref">Numbers 22:29, BBE</span>

![bg](./assets/bg-donkey.avif)

```notes
- tell it for the laugh: Balaam just argues with the talking donkey
- me by week two: amazed, then arguing, then short with it
- how fast it stopped being remarkable --- what I'd have got most wrong five years ago
- leave hanging: Balaam takes it in his stride; so did I. Should I have?
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

“are we to get water for you\
**out of the rock?**”

<span class="verse-ref">Numbers 20:10, BBE</span>

![bg](./assets/bg-rock.avif)

```notes
- told to *speak* to the rock (v8); strikes it twice instead, says "we"; water comes anyway; costs him the land
- two failures: force where he was given a word; claiming the power
- it works whether or not my heart's right; nothing in the tool says when I've started saying "we"
- which decisions get harder when making gets easier?
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

“And so the Word became flesh\
and **took a place among us** for a time”

<span class="verse-ref">John 1:14, BBE</span>

![bg](./assets/bg-word-flesh.avif)

```notes
- the verse you held in pieces, in the translation you held it in
- God speaks and things happen; then the Word doesn't send, he *comes*: one body, one town, a span of years
- my words reach further; I'm in fewer places. John 1:14 runs the other way --- being somewhere, at cost (foot washing, 13:3--5)
- **land this:** the Word became flesh; we've made our words a machine that takes no place at all
- I don't know yet what it's doing to me; I'd like to know what you make of it
```
