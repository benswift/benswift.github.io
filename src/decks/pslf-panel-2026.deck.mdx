```notes
- Lexing has given you the adoption curve; I want to zoom in on one segment of it
- the Industrial Revolution story we usually tell is a story about _making_ getting cheaper --- scale, repeatability, unit cost
- hold that thought, because it's the half of the story that doesn't transfer
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-long-gap.avif)

```notes
- before any of that scale kicked in, there was this: the gap between the idea and the first thing that actually worked
- enormous, slow, capital-intensive. Patternmakers, tooling, foundries, years of it
- so almost nobody got to cross it --- and the people who did were the people who could fund the crossing
- which means what got made was decided by who could afford to try
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-collapsed-gap.avif)

```notes
- agentic coding collapses that first gap to something you step over. Idea to working thing in an afternoon
- the _second_ gap --- working thing to robust, secure, maintained, at scale --- shrinks too, but nowhere near as much
- don't oversell this. The second chasm is real, and it's where most of the cost now lives
- but the first one was the one doing the gatekeeping
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-the-crit.avif)

```notes
- if crossing the first gap is nearly free, it stops being the constraint. The constraint moves to knowing _which_ of these to build
- deciding what good looks like in an open-ended field of possibilities --- and being able to defend the choice --- is now the whole box and dice
- (alt phrasing: when making is cheap, the whole job is choosing; and a choice you can't argue for isn't a decision, it's a default)
- that isn't a software skill. It's a studio skill. It's a crit --- you pin the work up and you say why this one
- for this room: less and less of the job is producing the artefact, more and more of it is judging the artefact and saying why
- so --- do we all need to go to art school? Partly, yes. What art school teaches is taste you can argue for, and that's the bit that's now scarce
- (Q&A hook: what art school _doesn't_ teach is how to cross the second chasm)
```
