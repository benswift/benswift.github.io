```notes
- pick up from Lexing's adoption curve, zoom in on one segment

- the price of the deal was fit: the factory's sizes, not the tailor's cut
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-off-the-rack.avif)

```notes
- software ran the same deal, harder: copying is free, the _first copy_ is the whole cost
- so SaaS: pay for the first copy once, sell it ten thousand times, everyone bends their process to fit
- this room's software estate is off the rack all the way down. It was the only deal on offer
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-made-to-measure.avif)

```notes
- agentic coding collapses the cost of the first copy: idea to working thing in an afternoon
- bespoke at the off-the-shelf price. This revolution runs the IR deal the other way: _exactly right_, cheap
- precise: the first working version gets cheap, not "software"
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-collapsed-gap.avif)

```notes
- don't oversell: the second chasm (robust, secure, maintained, integrated) shrinks much less
- in a university that's where the cost always was. The licence was never the expensive bit
- Jevons: cheap bespoke means _more_ software, not less. Ten thousand hand-made tools is a maintenance problem nobody has solved
```

---

{/* _class: hero */}

![bg](./assets/pslf-panel-2026/bg-the-crit.avif)

```notes
- making was the bottleneck; now the constraint is knowing what you want, and saying why
- the people who know how this place actually runs are in this room, not the IT shop
- it's a crit: pin it up, say why this one fits. A choice you can't argue for is a default
- less of the job is procuring the artefact, more of it is judging it
```

```notes
- closer: if you think AI is going to change the world, but you can't imagine it changing your default office productivity suite, you've got a much narrower idea of "changing the world" than you think
- (backup if needed: the CEO of the company that sells you that suite has said as much --- "why do I need Excel?")
```
